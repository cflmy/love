# QDQC Cinematic Direction

Shot bible for directors / AI / engineers. No implementation code here — only how each scene should be **filmed**.  
Source intent: `docs/007.md`.

---

## Global language

- Scroll drives **shots**, not section swaps.
- Prefer spatial change over opacity.
- Emotion > technical showcase.
- Quiet Days / Letter must drop spectacle.

---

## 相逢鹊渡 (first quality gate)

### Emotional purpose

First meeting of two beings in one world — user should feel: “they really share this space.” Not a character intro deck.

### Shots

| Progress | Shot | Camera | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.00–0.15 | Establishing | Wide, barely moves | Moon + mountains + water breathe |
| 0.15–0.30 | Butterfly enters | Drift left/down | Bezier flight into frame |
| 0.30–0.45 | Crossing | Track butterfly | Water / mist respond |
| 0.45–0.60 | Magpie reveal | Pull back slightly | Tiny distant silhouette grows |
| 0.60–0.75 | Approach | Slow dolly | Magpie nears bridge |
| 0.75–0.90 | Bridge awakening | Push toward bridge | Light sweep L→C→R |
| 0.90–1.00 | Meeting + title | Settle | Creatures meet; text blur→focus |

### Depth (target ≥10 layers)

sky · moon · far mountains · near mountains · mist · water · bridge · butterfly · magpie · foreground

### Motion character

Butterfly organic · Magpie graceful · Water continuous · Mist slow · Foreground fast

### Forbidden

Fade-only · static creatures · generic particles · CSS-only buttons · abrupt cuts

### Typography

「相逢鹊渡」 blur→focus late · 「相守情长」 delayed · 「故与君鹊渡情长」 last

---

## Opening / NFC

Darkness → tiny light → water → moon → bridge → butterfly. Do not fade all elements at once. CTA is part of the glowing bridge / card ritual.

---

## Past (前世)

Mythological / vast / dreamlike. Camera rises; cloud pass; color shift; modern dissolve. No hard cut into mythology.

---

## Present (今世)

Huge/slow/epic → small/warm/close. Mountains become room / tea / hands. World becomes human.

---

## Journey (山高路远)

Major cinematic sequence: sunset · return · waiting · phoenix light · running · embrace → **silence**. Dual approach. Do not implement as 10 stacked DOM fades.

---

## Quiet Days / Letter / Ending

Quiet Days: almost no scroll agitation. Letter may leave WebGL. Ending: settle, More Days Together, ∞ — restraint over fireworks.

---

## Image framing note

When “filming” with stills: show the illustration. Prefer letterboxing / contain over chopping. Cover is a deliberate close-up choice, not a default crop hammer.
